# Super Awesome Craft

A Minecraft total conversion for a small friends server. Vanilla's moment-to-moment play stays familiar; progression, economy and shared goals are replaced.

## Language

**Era**:
A stage of progression with its own area, materials and power tier. A player advances by clearing its Era Gate.
_Avoid_: Tier, chapter, age

**Era Gate**:
The dungeon chain that ends an Era. It contains group encounters and a climax fight in an arena built into the dungeon. Clearing it unlocks the next Era for that player.
_Avoid_: Boss, boss fight

**Climax fight**:
The final encounter of an Era Gate. It takes place in an arena the game designed, which players cannot relocate or cheese.
_Avoid_: Boss fight

**Personal unlock**:
Access a player gains by clearing an Era Gate themselves. Progression is personal by default.
_Avoid_: World unlock

**Catch-up easing**:
The reduced cost or difficulty of an Era for every player once anyone on the server has cleared its Gate. It eases progression but never requires it.
_Avoid_: World state, world progression

**Convenience**:
Quality-of-life capability such as durability, mining speed, storage, mobility and block access. Available early and without combat.
_Avoid_: Utility gear

**Power**:
Combat capability such as damage, protection and survivability. Earned through Eras.

**Settlement**:
A player-built place that Villagers move into and use. Its Prosperity unlocks trades and services, and it draws Raids.
_Avoid_: Village, colony, base

**Prosperity**:
A Settlement's level of development, derived from what has been built and how it's used.

**Raid**:
A wave-based group encounter against a Settlement that scales with its Prosperity. It never destroys player blocks.

**Motivation**:
A reason a player plays, such as huge projects, collecting or power. Players combine several; the design targets motivations, not archetypes.
_Avoid_: Archetype, player type, class

**Collection Log**:
A per-player record of distinct items, creatures and places discovered, with rewards for breadth.
