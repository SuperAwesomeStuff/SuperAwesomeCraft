# Personal progression with catch-up easing

**Status:** Accepted.

In the September 2026 playtest, every player climbed a private ladder. Fast players ran out of goals and left; slow players fell behind and left. Terraria's world-wide boss progression solves both problems, but forcing world state on everyone takes choices away from players who want to go at their own pace or ignore combat.

Progression is **personal**: clearing an Era Gate unlocks the next Era for the player who cleared it. The world only provides **catch-up easing**. Once anyone has cleared a Gate, that Era becomes cheaper or easier for everyone (for example: cheaper trades, easier-to-find structures, cheaper Catalysts). World progress never forces content on a player and never locks anything behind another player's actions.

## Consequences

- Leading players feel they're opening the way for others; trailing players get a real catch-up path.
- Builders and players who avoid combat can ignore Eras entirely without falling behind on Convenience.
- Each player's unlock state has to be tracked per player, and the world's easing state per Era.
