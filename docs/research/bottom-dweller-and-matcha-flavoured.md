# Research: The Bottom Dweller and Matcha Flavoured

Summary of design ideas from The Bottom Dweller's modpack reviews and Klei Wright's Matcha Flavoured, gathered November 2026 from video transcripts, the Matcha Flavoured repository and its wiki.

## The Bottom Dweller's criteria

- **Slower is not harder.** Taking longer is a time sink; real difficulty is a skill check.
- **No invisible progression walls.** Progress should never stop abruptly on something random world generation decides, with no tools to work around it.
- **Players will always optimize their time.** Don't ban the optimal route; make it less required so casual players can skip it while optimizers keep it.
- **Don't gate progress behind rare drops.** Diamonds and Netherite work because they can be mined reliably at a known depth.
- **Death should be punishing but fair.** A middle ground such as keeping the hotbar, armor and tools while dropping the rest.
- **Hunger is fun only when it gives a benefit, not as a chore.**
- **Vanilla adventuring is weak:** no step between horse and Elytra, none between Bundle and Shulker box (so players rush to the End), and key structures can't be found by learnable rules.
- **Additions need meaning.** Content whose rewards match strip-mining gets ignored; "Minecraft's charm is that it has a little something for everybody."
- **Gear should have identity:** materials with abilities instead of one best set.
- "Just because something is better with friends doesn't mean it's good."

## His Matcha Flavoured review (8/10)

**Praised:** a slower early game without artificial scarcity, alloys with abilities, reliable sources for luck-based items, no hunger with food giving buffs, advancements as a guide, and early craftable Shulkers.

**Criticized:** fast zombies as artificial difficulty, enchantments lost when upgrading tools, a death system that was both too forgiving and too harsh, the scarcity of silver, and finite spawners claimed first in multiplayer.

## Matcha Flavoured (Klei Wright)

A datapack plus resource pack (optional mod) for Java 26.1.2–26.3. Its goal: slow-paced players should gather as much as technical players in the same time.

- Horizontal alloys so "every player [has] a different idea on what the best set is."
- Crafted enchanting with inputs from spawners and bosses instead of mob grinders.
- A scarce currency (obols) with few buyer Villagers: "substitute one form of grinding for another."
- No hunger and no natural regeneration; Keep Inventory, but each death costs a max heart.
- The surface becomes safe after the Dragon, so the late game feels different.
- Klei's appendix: players propose solutions that aren't always the real fix; balance for your audience, not for optimizers.

## Relevance

- Several viable income routes, with farms optional, not banned.
- Pacing through skill checks and encounters rather than time sinks.
- Early Convenience (Shulkers, durability) so adventuring and building don't wait for the endgame.
- Avoid first-come finite resources on shared servers.
